# Director Panel

Press **D** to toggle the director panel drawer. The panel displays runtime information and offers simple controls.

## BGM
- Select a song from the dropdown.
- Use the play/pause buttons to control playback.
- Adjust volume with the slider.

## Video
- Choose one of the sample videos from the list.
- Toggle visibility with the checkbox.

## Lighting
- Switch between `idle`, `dramatic` and `calm` presets.

## Camera
- Quickly switch camera angle: `wide`, `closeUp` or `sideView`.
